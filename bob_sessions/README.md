# Bob Session Summaries (required evidence)

The hackathon **requires** screenshots of your Bob task session summaries in
this folder. An entry without them may not be judged.

## How to capture one

1. In Bob IDE's chat, open the **Tasks** list.
2. Click the task related to your submission.
3. Click the task **header** — a session consumption summary appears.
4. Screenshot it (PNG), and save it here.

## Naming

Use: `minahiru_taskNN_short-description_summary.png`

Examples:
- `minahiru_task01_env_scan_summary.png`
- `minahiru_task02_doc_check_summary.png`
- `minahiru_task03_verification_summary.png`

Capture one right after each real task — don't leave it to the end.
